In Islamic tradition, there's this incredibly nuanced term—'Baraka.'While often translated as 'blessing,' its implications stretch far beyond that simple label. It's closely tied to the idea of 'sufficiency,' of 'having enough.' In a capitalist world that thrives on the fear of scarcity, the concept of Baraka offers a poignant counter-narrative. The reality is that God determines what 'enough' truly is, making even the smallest of things expansive in their reach. You might not possess much on the surface, but with Baraka infusing what you do have, it becomes more than enough. And that is the epitome of beautiful.
