Human beings can be, and often are, incredibly fake and inauthentic. They will lie, cheat, and steal. They will make false promises, betray you, and potentially rob you of your life, both literally and figuratively. Humans can very well cause you the most acute pain of your entire spiritual career. And the crux of all this is that we have no choice but to persist in loving, understanding, being patient with, and recognizing that all of those things are within us. We have to deal with monsters not just outside but within us as well. This is the truth. There are monsters out there, but there are monsters inside us also. This experience of being human is about being able to look in the mirror and see the Light -and then realizing that everyone we look at is indeed a mirror, and that beneath their apparent ugliness is indeed Light.
