Prayer is the refuge. Prayer is truly all we have. To raise our hands in hopeful submission, to plea, to beg, to feel the depth of brokenness—this is the only course of action for us limited human beings. When we put our empty hands together and raise them up toward heaven, we invite much beyond words. We transcend time and space, entering a mode of being full of mystery. We attempt and aspire to communicate our needs, our wants, and right now, that is for Palestine and its honorable people to be showered with infinite mercy and protection. We cannot underestimate the power of Prayer. It is all we have.
