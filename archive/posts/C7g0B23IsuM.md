I think what we are all seeking can be boiled down to two things. I think we're seeking sacred space that is also safe. A space where we can navigate our journey to God without the fear of judgement, a space where we can come as we are and not have to pretend to be something we are not. A lot of sacred spaces in our current day and age sadly do not echo this level of safety. The second thing we're seeking is people, not just any type of people but the people that can lead us toward better, people who remind us of Light through just their behaviour, people who Love but also teach us what it means to become Beloved. I think this is really what we need -it is what we seek so deeply.
