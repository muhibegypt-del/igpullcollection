It is never too late to start a relationship with your Lord. It is never too late to begin looking after your heart. It is never too late for us to turn back and go back Home. It is never too late for you to pick up the prayer mat and unroll it. It is never too late for us to learn more about our religion. Remember; It is never too late to fall in love with God.
