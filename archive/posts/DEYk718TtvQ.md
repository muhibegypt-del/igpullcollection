Muhib @muhibthewriter Growing up means learning to let people be who they are. We can talk to them, try to help them, or show them a better way, but we can't make them change. It sounds simple, but it's really hard. Sometimes we want people to act a certain way because we need them to. Other times, it's just to make ourselves feel better. But no matter what, we can't control other people. In the end, relationships are about trust. We trust each other, and that's what makes them work. Maybe that's why it feels scary sometimes. We have faith in each other and when it falls apart it breaks us, but when it works it fills us with love. This is the story of beinghuman.
