I remember asking her, What is prayer? She gave me an answer from the heart, but it never satisfied my mind. Are we Muslim? She nodded, but with quiet reservation. My mum was one when colonial rule ended, leaving her without an Islamic education. Many migrants who came to the UK in the 60s were in their early teens—forced to work, not study religion.
