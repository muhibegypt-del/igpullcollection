I can be as religious as I want. I can grow the biggest beard, wear the finest perfume, and attend the mosque more than anyone else. But if my character is bad, what does it all mean? I've been thinking about this a lot. It's interesting how often a person's character is overlooked. Being rude, short, or outright offensive while thinking religious acts will save you is a path to delusion. This tradition is about character—its perfection and the upholding of virtue. It's not just appearances. Yet we've been misled into thinking it's about performing acts for rewards while ignoring the people around us.
