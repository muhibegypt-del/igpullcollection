A teacher once told me never to give up. He said this journey to God is full of trials, temptations, and moments of weakness, but God's mercy never ends. It's never-ending mercy! Sometimes we fall short, make mistakes, look at things we shouldn't, say things we shouldn't, engage in habits that damage our souls, or worse, those around us. Yet, nowhere does it say that God won't forgive us. In fact, it's the opposite: God says, "Turn back, and I will forgive you. Come back, and I will let you restart." This is beyond beautiful. Words fall short— they can't fully capture how immense this is. Sometimes, the worst thing is forgetting that God's mercy is always ready to cover our faults. We give up, thinking we need to rely on ourselves, but instead, we can aspire to rely on His mercy.
