The saddest part of jealousy is that it often comes from those closest to us. What's even more challenging is realizing that our achievements are not solely our own, but gifts from God. In this sense, jealousy isn't just about disliking someone's success; it's about disliking that God chose to bless them. This is why jealousy is so insidious, and why it's considered a cardinal sin— it suggests that God made a mistake in giving that blessing. It's a darkness without light. And it hurts the most, when it's directed to you from people closest to you.
