There are officially less than 60 days until the blessed month of Ramadan arrives. I don't feel ready; in fact, I feel quite the opposite, and I know many others share this feeling. But that's the reality, isn't it? The truth is, readiness or not, humility is essential. Humility is the secret. A saint once said that Satan can compete with us in everything – in knowledge, in fasting, in many things. But, the saint added, there is one thing he cannot compete with us in: humility. I find this so profound. Whether you're ready for Ramadan or dreading it, the key lies in a sense of brokenness, a sense of emptiness. This, to me, is truly beautiful.
