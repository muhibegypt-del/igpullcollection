I remember once complaining about my age. I was maybe seven, and I proclaimed that I wished I was thirty. The adults at the dinner table burst into laughter, practically entering into hysterics. They urged me to repeat it, and then cackled together, huddled over in amusement. At the time, I had no clue; I just assumed they found it hilarious. I thought, wow, thirty must be amazing, look how happy it makes them. Now that I'm thirty, I can confirm they were not laughing out of joy. They were laughing at innocence, all harboring a secret desire to be seven again.
