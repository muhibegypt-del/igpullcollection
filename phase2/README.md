# Phase 2: a collection of short pieces

This is an editorial shortlist of the Phase 1 archive, not a manuscript or a claim that all recovered text is ready for print. The proposed form is a collection to browse and revisit: short prose, scenes, reflections, and occasional one-line pieces. Theme labels help find work; they are not mandatory chapter divisions.

## What was assessed

All **504 posts** in the archive received a first editorial status in [ASSESSMENT.csv](ASSESSMENT.csv):

These are counts of recovered archive text, not polished book text. OCR can include handles, repeated headers, unattributed material, or speech; further editing can reduce the totals. A small number of exceptionally short pieces also means post count is not a proxy for page count.

## Selection judgment

The strongest pieces tend to contain an observed scene, a personal admission, an image that survives beyond the social post, or a precise turn of thought. The shortlist deliberately mixes these with a few small aphorisms. Repeated expressions of hope, healing, sincerity, and grace can become monotonous in a book, so one strong instance generally beats several near copies. A piece was kept short where its brevity is its strength.

The richest longer pieces include the father's open-door memory, the encounter in Morocco, the first attempt at prayer, and portraits of friendship, grief, and ordinary kindness. The archive also has topical commentary whose setting matters; those pieces need context and fact checking before print. The justice selection is small because the first pass favoured self-contained writing rather than trying to manufacture equal theme quotas.

This is a **provisional** selection. `core` means worth developing, not publication approved. The CSV records the current status, recovered word count, primary Phase 1 theme, an individual editorial note, and review flags. Source posts and media remain in their original folders.

## Is there a substantial book here?

There is enough material to make a compelling **short collection** or a sample for a publisher or designer. The 88-piece core, at roughly 11,900 recovered words, would be a slim book. Core plus reserve is roughly 27,300 recovered words before editing. That is a plausible path to a fuller collection, but only after the reserve is checked and cut; page design cannot supply missing substance.

Several later posts appear to be screenshots or teasers for full Substack essays. Their full texts are **not** in this repository. Getting those originals, if they are yours and you want them included, is the clearest way to test a more substantial edition. New bridging essays could also be written, but the collection does not need a single thesis or explanatory filler.

## Source and rights checks before publication

- Captions and publication dates were absent from the source download. The `archive/posts/` Markdown is automated transcription for research. Recheck any chosen text against its images or videos, especially quoted speech and carousel joins.
- Remove or attribute external quotations, screenshots, translated passages, and passages by other writers. A few obvious third-party posts were kept out of the shortlist, but authorship should be reviewed across the whole selection.
- Check names, identifying details, and permission where a piece describes a private person. Check factual claims in topical pieces. Confirm the preferred rendering of Arabic terms, divine names, and Qur'anic or hadith references.
- Confirm whether older `@muhiblog` and `@relytsikcaj` work is yours, and whether the same text appeared elsewhere. Similar posts should enter the book only once.

## Interior study

[book-preview.tex](book-preview.tex) is a standalone, book-size LaTeX sample section called **The Door Was Open**. It sequences seven selected pieces around welcome and mercy while leaving each piece self-contained. This is a sample of editorial proximity, not a rule that the whole collection must follow one argument. [PREVIEW_NOTES.md](PREVIEW_NOTES.md) maps each piece back to its original and records the light copyedits. The source images for all seven pieces were checked during preparation.

[book-preview-visual.pdf](book-preview-visual.pdf) is a visual companion made from the same editorial text at the intended trim size. It is **not** a LaTeX compile and may differ slightly in line and page breaks. The app's LaTeX compiler failed to initialize with a platform-directory error, before reading this source; compile the `.tex` file in a working LaTeX environment before treating its layout as verified.

The next editorial pass is to read the 88 core pieces together, remove repetition that only becomes visible in sequence, decide which reserves add something distinct, recover any full essays, and then settle the length and order.
