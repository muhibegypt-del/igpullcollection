# Interior study: source and edit notes

**The Door Was Open** is an illustrative sequence of seven short pieces. The first preview spread its attention across too many unrelated subjects. This version follows an image of welcome: a neighbour's open home, room for a beginner, mercy without a gate, hospitality to a visitor and a convert, help from strangers, and shared hope. Each piece can still be read on its own. The section title and individual titles are editorial suggestions, not the author's original titles.

The prose comes from the linked posts. Each source image was visually checked, including both Morocco carousels.
